## Direction contract
THESIS: Lagos sites hide behind hoardings and announce themselves on a contractor signboard. The page is that hoarding: every section is raw site behind and finished work in front, split by a seam you drag, so "anyone can put a building up, he makes it beautiful" happens under the visitor's hand. Refuses the single before/after slider tucked into one section.

Finished: white marble ground and the full-colour photo, set in a high-contrast display serif. A printed readout states how finished the frame is as the seam moves (raise from the terminal: states print themselves). Big Shoulders Stencil and Gloock, with Figtree for reading.
